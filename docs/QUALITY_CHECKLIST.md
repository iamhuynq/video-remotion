# Video quality checklist

Use this before calling a video ready to publish. Not every item needs an automated test, but each should be reviewed.

## Content

- [ ] Names, rankings, dates, and factual claims are correct.
- [ ] The hook communicates a clear question, promise, or surprising idea.
- [ ] Every beat adds information, emotion, or a payoff.
- [ ] Text is concise and consistent in tone and spelling.
- [ ] The ending resolves the premise or gives a specific next step.

## Visuals

- [ ] Canvas and aspect ratio are correct for the target platform.
- [ ] No missing, broken, stretched, or unexpectedly cropped images.
- [ ] Faces and key silhouettes are unobstructed.
- [ ] Text stays within safe margins and has sufficient contrast.
- [ ] Text is readable at phone size and remains on screen long enough.
- [ ] No accidental blank/black frames, jumps, flicker, or one-frame artifacts.
- [ ] Motion is smooth and intentional; important reveals do not move too quickly.
- [ ] Transitions do not obscure the subject or create confusing visual continuity.

## Audio

- [ ] Narration is complete and intelligible.
- [ ] Music does not overpower speech.
- [ ] Sound effects align with the visual events.
- [ ] No clipping, abrupt unwanted cuts, or unintended silence.
- [ ] Audio and picture start/end at the intended times.

## Technical

- [ ] `npm run lint` passes.
- [ ] Remotion Studio preview works.
- [ ] Still preview is inspected.
- [ ] Final MP4 is inspected at normal playback speed.
- [ ] Duration, frame rate, and dimensions match the intended output.
- [ ] Render errors or warnings have been investigated rather than ignored.

## Publishing and rights

- [ ] Image, font, music, and sound-effect sources are documented.
- [ ] Usage rights and platform rules have been checked.
- [ ] No accidental debug labels, placeholders, local paths, or production notes appear in the video.
- [ ] Final filename and output path are correct.
